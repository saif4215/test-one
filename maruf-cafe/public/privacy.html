Privacy Policy

Maruf Cafe · 365 Veterans Rd W, Staten Island, NY 10309 · Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what the Maruf Cafe app and website collect, why, and what we do with it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into our forms when you ask for a large-order quote or an event rental:

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • Your request: dates and times, number of people, delivery address if you ask for delivery, food items, allergies or dietary needs, budget, special requests and any notes you add.

You can use the rest of the app (menu, hours, contact details, the group-size planner and your order list) without entering any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

What we do not collect

  • No payment card details. Card payments, if you choose to pay, happen on Square, not in this app.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking. We do not sell or share your information for advertising.

How we use it

We use your request only to contact you about your order or event, to prepare a quote, and to answer your questions.

Who can see it

Your request is sent to the Maruf Cafe server and stored there. Maruf Cafe staff can read it, and it may be forwarded to them by email or a chat tool the café uses. Our hosting provider processes it on our behalf to run the service. We do not sell your information.

The AI assistant

If the café has switched on the assistant, the questions you type in "Ask Maruf Cafe" are sent to our server and from there to Anthropic, the company that runs the Claude AI model, which writes the answer. We do not store the chat. Please do not type card numbers, passwords or other sensitive information. The assistant can make mistakes: Maruf Cafe confirms prices, availability and allergy information with you directly.

How long we keep it

We keep requests for as long as we need them to respond to you and to keep ordinary business records. You can ask us to delete your request at any time.

Links to other services

The app links to other sites you may choose to open, such as our Square Online store, Instagram and TikTok. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you sent us, contact us using the details below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here and update the date above.
